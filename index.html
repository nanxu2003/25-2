Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to implement an IoT solution?
This depends on the complexity of the project. Standardised data acquisition and monitoring systems are typically deployed within 1-2 weeks. For larger projects involving customised development, the timeline is usually 1-3 months. We provide a detailed project schedule during the requirements gathering phase.
Can your equipment be used if there's no broadband network on-site?
Absolutely. Our gateway devices support 4G/5G full-network modules. For remote areas or scenarios where cabling is difficult, we can also utilise LoRa or NB-IoT low-power wide-area network technologies for wireless transmission.
Do you provide after-sales support?
We offer a 1-year free warranty and remote technical support. After the warranty period expires, you can opt to sign a maintenance service contract to enjoy regular inspections, software upgrades, and priority fault resolution services.
